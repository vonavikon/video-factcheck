video-factcheck logo: four concepts

The sign for a YouTube fact-checking extension. Per research on fact-checking organizations (Snopes, PolitiFact, Full Fact, Reuters, AFP): a shield and a magnifier are not the fact-check genre, and the play triangle is free in this genre. We do not take red, because a red triangle reads as YouTube; green and yellow are taken by verdicts.

Verdict colors stay with the interface: the sign is neutral.

Dark background (extension panel)

A · Play-check
play triangle + checkmark · recommended
Video and checking in one sign. The niche is free in fact-checking; it reads from 16 px the way product icons do.

B · Subtitle line
transcript plate + checkmark
The most product-like mechanic: subtitles where a word gives way to a verdict. At 16 px the plate blurs together, so this is a sign for large placements.
48 32 16
C · Lens-play
lens with a triangle + checkmark
A double metaphor of "search inside a video". A minus from the research: a dominant magnifier is the search and plagiarism-check genre, not fact-checking.

D · Frame with a checkmark · CHOSEN
video frame outline + checkmark · chosen variant D
The most neutral and safe option. Minus: a checkbox-player hybrid with no character of its own; dozens of todo apps look like this.

Light background (Chrome Web Store, documents)

A · "ink" variant
if the blue feels foreign
The extension panel color. Strict, but on the dark panel background it needs a light backing plate.

The chosen sign is variant D: a video frame outline plus a checkmark, blue #2F6FED. The extension icons 16/32/48/128 are built from D (a bold variant for the small grid); the sign sits on the button under the video and on the collapsed dot.
