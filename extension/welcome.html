YouTube Fact-Check

Welcome! Here is what the extension does and why it needs its permissions.

What the extension does

It fact-checks claims from YouTube videos while you watch and shows a verdict — true, false or unclear — with links to sources.

  1. Open any video on YouTube. A "Fact-check" button appears below it.
  2. Click it — a panel opens next to the video, and a verdict map appears on the player timeline.
  3. Read the explanations and open the sources: scientific papers, official sites, published fact-checks.

This is an AI assessment with sources — not moderation and not a channel rating.

Permissions and why

The extension asks for exactly three permissions — here is what each one does:

PermissionWhy
youtube.com Works on video pages: the "Fact-check" button and the panel appear on youtube.com only. The extension stays silent everywhere else.
fact-check server Fact-check server: claims from the subtitles are sent to the checking server — it searches for sources and returns verdicts. Without this access no checking is possible.
storage Settings and install ID: the extension stores locally in your browser the check language, the free-check balance and the history of recent checks. The install ID is a random ID with no personal data.

Where to start

Open any video with subtitles and click the "Fact-check" button below it. You get 10 free machine checks every day; matches with human-made fact-checks are always free.
